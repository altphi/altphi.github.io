
The seven rules of organization

1.  Everything has a home.
2.  Don't sacrifice work space for storage.
3.  Proximity follow priority.
4.  Don't organize junk (toss it).
5.  Visibility beats memory (keep in sight or add labels).
6.  One motion is better than two.
7.  Build for flexibility.
