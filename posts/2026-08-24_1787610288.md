
Test your typing speed at [monkeytype](https://monkeytype.com/).

And these TUIs are good:
- [ttyper](https://github.com/max-niederman/ttyper)
- [toofan](https://github.com/vyrx-dev/toofan)

The latter has a number of programming languages included too.

[gtypist](https://www.gnu.org/software/gtypist/) is best for practicing a wide variety of movements by means of well organized tutorials.

There's also [keybr.com](https://keybr.com/) which seems to be in the same
spirit but adapts its exercises based on mistakes and performance.
